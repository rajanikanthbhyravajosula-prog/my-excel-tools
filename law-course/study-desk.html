How to answer and how marks are given

One-line answers (1 mark)

  • Give the answer and its section or Article.
  • Example: "90 days, CPC S.148A(5)".
  • The section usually carries part of the mark.

Short notes (3–4 marks)

  1. Define it, citing the section.
  2. List the essentials or conditions.
  3. Cite one case.
  4. Give an example.
  5. One-line conclusion.

Problems (IRAC method)

  1. Issue: the legal question.
  2. Rule: the sections and cases.
  3. Application: apply them to the facts.
  4. Conclusion: your answer.

How marks are given

Each written answer has a marking scheme of key points, each worth part of the marks. Multiple-choice questions are marked instantly. Written answers are marked by Claude against the model answer, and you can adjust any mark.
